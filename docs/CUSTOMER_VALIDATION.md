# Validate with 5 renovation business owners

Status: **not yet conducted**. This is an interview worksheet, not claimed market evidence.

Start with: “How many quotations do you send per month, and how many do you forget to follow up?”

Then ask:
1. Walk me through the last quotation that never received a reply. What happened next?
2. How do you track follow-ups now: memory, WhatsApp, Excel, notebook, or CRM?
3. Who follows up, how often, and what causes the task to be missed?
4. Would an email follow-up be useful, or must it be WhatsApp? Why?
5. What would make you distrust automatic messaging?
6. Could you try this with a few real quotes after checking each message? What would make the trial worthwhile?
7. What do you currently spend in time or money solving this problem? Discuss willingness to pay only after understanding the workflow.
